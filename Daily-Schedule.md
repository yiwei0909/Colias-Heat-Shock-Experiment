3.	For each container: 
-Check condition – fill the [form](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Q1tdF-AWX9o_B0E2gq9r0EQPBRKgEuWP/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=101366657459124488713&rtpof=true&sd=true) (Instar growth).
-Measure and take pictures – upload pictures [here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1jMQi1wBJAeLQL0VAXUa3PqHEOlpCiN76?usp=drive_link) and sort it.
-Measure their weight since from 3rd stage.
-Fully wipe and clean container
-Change new fresh clover and place the caterpillar on fresh leaf.
